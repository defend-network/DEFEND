# DEFENDMarkets — Aggressive Commercialization & Quant Intelligence Plan

**90-Day Signal → Live Edge → OPEX Clear → Scale / Cut**  
**Owner Directive / Strategy Lock**

## Mission

Turn DEFENDMarkets from a research and monitoring system into a continuously learning quantitative market-intelligence operation capable of:

1. discovering genuine predictive edge;
2. proving that edge prospectively;
3. converting validated edge into executable live results;
4. continuously improving its algorithms and data;
5. covering its own operating costs;
6. scaling only when measured economics justify scale.

The operating philosophy is **fast learning + hard measurement + aggressive experimentation + controlled capital**.

This is not slow academic research, six months of paper analysis before useful action, an LLM guessing probabilities, blind automation, stake escalation to force profitability, backtest theater, or accuracy theater. This is compressed evidence generation. We intend to learn in 90 days what a weak program might take a year to learn.

## Core operating principles

### Real data or abstain

Never fabricate odds, results, prices, market probabilities, provider coverage, fills, P&L, CLV, model outputs, or player history. If required evidence is unavailable, state **UNAVAILABLE**. Do not infer a tradable opportunity from missing data.

### The AI directs research; code proves the result

GPT-5.6 Terra is the persistent supervisory quantitative intelligence. Terra may inspect, question, diagnose, form hypotheses, request additional data, design features and experiments, train challengers through authorized research tools, run ablations and walk-forward validation, compare algorithms, investigate drift, identify model weaknesses, recommend strategy changes, reject weak approaches, promote qualifying challengers to SHADOW or PAPER, and maintain the research queue.

The deterministic statistical/research engine performs actual training, probability calculation, metrics, backtesting, walk-forward testing, calibration measurement, bootstrap/confidence estimation, settlement, and P&L accounting. Terra interprets mathematics; Terra does not replace mathematics.

### M5 is the initial champion, not a religion

Initial research/production champion: `M5_REGULARIZED_LOGISTIC`. M5 remains frozen and reproducible until a challenger objectively beats it.

Future lineage is **CHAMPION → M5** with challengers `M6`, `M7`, `M8`, etc. Candidate families may include regularized logistic variants, enhanced Elo systems, Bayesian ratings, gradient boosted trees, calibrated ensembles, market-prior models, hybrid statistical models, ensembles, and other justified methods. No model is promoted because it sounds sophisticated. Evidence decides.

### Champion / challenger always

Never overwrite the current champion during research. New ideas create immutable challengers. Each challenger must preserve dataset snapshot, training cutoff, feature schema and timing, model family, hyperparameters, code version, validation windows, evaluation and calibration results, research rationale, AI interpretation, and promotion decision. The previous champion remains available for rollback.

### Live capital is the final test

Backtest success is evidence. Paper success is stronger evidence. CLV is stronger prospective evidence. Tiny live execution is stronger evidence still. Actual scalable edge requires **model edge × price availability × fill quality × limits × bankroll × risk discipline**. A theoretical edge that cannot be executed is not commercial edge.

## Commercial break-even

Approximate monthly return required:

| Bankroll | Clear $300 | Clear $500 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $3,000 | 10.0% | 16.7% |
| $5,000 | 6.0% | 10.0% |
| $10,000 | 3.0% | 5.0% |
| $20,000 | 1.5% | 2.5% |

Below roughly $5,000 deployable capital, fixed software expense creates a high return hurdle. Around $8,000–$12,000 deployable capital, moderate monthly edge can begin paying meaningful operating costs. Larger bankroll becomes constrained by limits, fill availability, account restrictions, liquidity, bookmaker tolerance, and edge decay. Bankroll is not the same as scalability.

Primary commercial metric is **NET_EXPECTED_VALUE_CAPTURE**, not raw ROI alone. Track gross and net P&L, expected P&L, realized-vs-expected, CLV, fill slippage, reject rate, capital utilization, opportunity capture, cost per settled decision, and cost per valid opportunity.

## Continuous intelligence loop

`OBSERVE → DIAGNOSE → FORM HYPOTHESIS → IDENTIFY REQUIRED DATA → VERIFY/ACQUIRE DATA → ENGINEER CANDIDATE FEATURE OR MODEL → CREATE EXPERIMENT → TRAIN CHALLENGER → BACKTEST → WALK-FORWARD VALIDATE → CALIBRATE → ABLATION → STRESS TEST → COMPARE TO CHAMPION + MARKET → PROMOTE/REJECT → SHADOW → PAPER → LIVE MICRO-RISK → SETTLEMENT → LEARN → repeat.`

Automatic triggers include new events, new odds, new results, large model errors, calibration drift, provider anomalies, model-market disagreement, daily performance/anomaly digest, weekly quant-research cycle, and monthly commercial/architecture review.

## AI research authority

Terra may autonomously investigate player-strength representations, recent-form windows, recency decay, fatigue, same-day match count, rest and schedule density, opponent quality, strength of schedule, H2H interactions, competition class, venue/time/travel factors when justified and available, market-implied probability, line movement, book disagreement, price dispersion, favorite/underdog regimes, volatility/upset propensity, graph/network strength, recent opponent strength, rating uncertainty, and missingness patterns.

For candidate external data sources record `DATA_SOURCE`, `EXPECTED_SIGNAL`, `COVERAGE`, `COST`, `LATENCY`, `HISTORICAL_DEPTH`, `IDENTITY_MATCHABILITY`, `LEGAL/ACCESS_STATUS`, `EXPECTED_FEATURES`, `EXPECTED_VALUE`, and `RECOMMENDATION`. Do not automatically buy expensive data outside owner-approved spend policy.

## Statistical toolkit

The Research Lab should support descriptive/distribution statistics, correlation and mutual information where appropriate, bootstrap/confidence intervals, Brier score, log loss, ROC-AUC when meaningful, calibration curves/intercept/slope/ECE, reliability diagrams, residual analysis, feature ablation, permutation importance, SHAP or equivalent when suitable, time-aware cross-validation, rolling/walk-forward validation, regime segmentation, drift detection, sensitivity analysis, Monte Carlo bankroll/drawdown/stake-policy simulation, market-price comparison, CLV/slippage analysis, and expected-vs-realized analysis. Accuracy alone never qualifies a model.

## Anti-leakage doctrine

Every feature used for prediction at time T must have existed at time T. Never use future match results, post-commence information in prematch predictions, future odds, settlement results, later identity corrections, future ratings/tournament information, or closing prices unavailable at prediction time. Preserve `EVENT_TIME`, `OBSERVATION_TIME`, `RECEIVED_TIME`, `PREDICTION_TIME`, and `CUTOFF_TIME`. Leakage audit is mandatory for every challenger promotion.

## Aggressive 90-day force plan

Calendar time does not automatically authorize capital escalation. Every phase has a **time target + evidence gate**. If evidence arrives sooner, advance sooner. If evidence fails, do not advance merely because the calendar says so.

### Days 0–7 — System alive

Complete live/forward TT ingestion, canonical participant identity, valid prematch odds, frozen M5 inference, prediction timestamps, append-only journal, settlement, provider health, model-performance calculation, Terra connected through authenticated research tools, admin-only AI chat, Terra research journal, and experiment/challenger registry. Owner must be able to ask what is happening now and why M5 produced a probability, receiving real model/market evidence, uncertainty, and counter-thesis. **Real risk: $0.**

### Days 8–14 — Signal or death

Target 150+ paper decisions if volume supports it. Required row fields: event, prediction time, model version/P, no-vig market P, edge, paper price, last-valid-prematch price, result, CLV, paper P&L. Required metrics: N, NO_ACTION rate, Brier, log loss, calibration, model-vs-market Brier, CLV distribution, paper/expected ROI, max paper drawdown, coverage, abstention reasons. Terra produces top model/data weaknesses, top five research hypotheses and feature ideas, champion confidence, and next experiments. No live capital if measurement/leakage/journal gates fail.

### Days 15–21 — Challenger sprint

Minimum five meaningful experiments if data supports them: a feature ablation, alternative calibration, alternative rating/strength formulation, nonlinear challenger, and market-informed challenger or explicit rejection test. Every experiment records hypothesis, rationale, snapshot, cutoff, model, feature set, validation method, result, uncertainty, ablation, leakage check, Terra interpretation, and KEEP/KILL decision. M5 remains champion unless objectively beaten.

### Days 22–35 — Micro live fire

Owner-defined test bankroll; suggested initial band $1,000–$3,000. Prefer fixed fractional units, initially about 0.25%–0.50% bankroll per independent position, with no full Kelly. Live entry requires freshness, identity, model, valid price, edge-after-costs, stake policy, portfolio exposure, daily-risk and approved-model gates. Log intended/filled price and time, stake, reject/limit/slippage/void/book/model/policy. Goal is to learn whether real fills resemble paper economics; freeze size on unexplained divergence or bugs.

### Days 36–50 — Prove edge quality

Terra performs calibration, CLV, execution, regime, feature, model attribution, and failure review. Segment by competition, history depth, probability/edge/price bucket, bookmaker, time-to-start, model confidence, and feature completeness. Identify where edge exists and where it does not. Become more selective over time.

### Days 51–60 — OPEX clear attempt

Move rolling commercial economics toward `MARKETS_NET >= $0`, preferably `>= $300/30 days`, where Markets net equals real betting profit minus Markets OPEX. Scale capture only if earlier evidence gates pass and drawdown/CLV/calibration/execution remain healthy. Improve through data, timing, books, selectivity, better challengers, and measured secondary markets—not forced risk.

### Days 61–75 — Scale winners / kill losers

Terra classifies model components, strategies, data sources, markets, bookmakers, features, and provider expenses as SCALE / KEEP / RESEARCH / NARROW / KILL. Remove weak complexity and focus capital where evidence is strongest.

### Days 76–90 — Systemize commercial loop

Require written playbook, automated monitoring, champion/challenger lifecycle, research queue, daily AI oversight, weekly risk committee, model lineage, capital rules, provider failover, rollback, cost accounting, and performance attribution. Day-90 decision is SCALE / MAINTAIN / NARROW / RESEARCH RESET / STOP. No ambiguous “keep trying” state.

## Model promotion ladder

`RESEARCH → BACKTEST → WALK_FORWARD → SHADOW → PAPER → MICRO_LIVE → CHAMPION`.

Terra may autonomously promote through PAPER when objective gates pass. Material changes to real-money model authority or stake policy require explicit owner approval for now. This may be relaxed only after the governance system itself proves reliable.

Promotion requires time-respecting validation, better or non-inferior Brier/log loss, acceptable calibration, stability across windows, no leakage, reasonable sample, no dependence on one tiny subgroup, ablation support, realistic cost robustness, and favorable market comparison where available. Prefer better calibration + edge identification + stability over raw accuracy.

## Market relationship and CLV

The market is information, not merely an opponent. Track `MODEL_P`, `MARKET_NO_VIG_P`, `MODEL_MINUS_MARKET`, `OPEN_PRICE`, `PRICE_AT_DECISION`, and `LAST_VALID_PREMATCH_PRICE`. Study where M5 beats market, market beats M5, disagreement contains edge, and disagreement predicts failure. Market probability may become a feature/prior/ensemble/calibration anchor only when walk-forward evidence supports it.

CLV uses the **last valid prematch price**, never post-commence prices relabeled as close. Track mean/median/positive-rate CLV by model, edge bucket, book, and competition. Profit must eventually confirm the process.

## Capital / risk policy

Separate tools budget from risk bankroll. Initial tools budget is roughly $250–$300/month. Longer-term aggressive deployable target may reach $8,000–$12,000 only after evidence supports it.

Initial recommended micro-live defaults: max single position 0.50% bankroll; max highly correlated exposure 1.0%; daily realized-loss soft stop about 2%; weekly about 5%; system drawdown review trigger about 10%; hard capital kill/owner review about 15%. These are starting governance defaults, not universal truths. Terra should later recommend policy from Monte Carlo simulations using observed edge, variance, correlation, frequency, calibration and drawdown. Never increase risk to recover losses.
